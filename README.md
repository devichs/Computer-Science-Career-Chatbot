CareerBot - A chatbot developed to assist students in identifying a Computer Science career Path.

The CareerBot chatbot is developed to interact with students interested in a Computer Science career and help them identify jobs based on their interests in the field.  To get them started CareerBot provides information on five career paths and lets the student choose any of the five paths based on their interests and responses to the guided chatbot conversation.

CareerBot was developoed using: 
https://home.pandorabots.com/home.html

These five computing jobs are recommended by CareerBot: 

1.	Computer and Information Research Scientists
2.	Computer Network Architects
3.	Software Developers, Quality Assurance Analysts, and Testers
4.	Database Administrators and Architects
5.	Information Security Analysts

Training cases were developed since no other case data was available. The training cases were presented as general options to a user based on the users’ interests. These general options were related to each of the five job types presented in step B. AIML was used to develop a decision tree based on how the user answers each question presented by CareerBot.

The AIML is structured with a greeting, a decision to assess career interests, choosing a career interest, then reviewing more information or returning to the decision to choose another career or end the conversation.

As the user is presented career types, whether they have a career type in mind or not, they can begin researching any of the career types and then return to pick another career type if desired. For example, a user begins a chat with CareerBot and starts the interest assessment. The user is presented with these career type areas: Research, Networking, Software, Data, and Security. If a user picks Research, they will be presented with a description of that career type and link to the Occupational Outlook Handbook where they can perform further research. If that sequence satisfies the user, they can then end the conversation, or they can choose to go back to the list of five career types and choose another career area for review.
